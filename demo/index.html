keybry

Open this page on your phone, then tap the message box at the bottom.

The bar moves with the keyboard, not after it. It guesses the keyboard height the moment you tap, then measures the real one and saves it for next time.

The pill at the top shows what it knows: phase · height · where the number came from (predicted, measured or exact). Tap it to see how this browser does it.

Close the keyboard and tap the box again. The second time there is nothing to correct.

Got two minutes? Run the test at the top. It records what your browser reports and turns it into a test case, so the next release works on your phone too.

On a computer? Open this page on a phone. Nothing moves on a desktop, because there is no on-screen keyboard.

Open a GitHub issue

The issue form opens with your answers filled in. Attach the downloaded file to it. The trace holds viewport sizes, event timings and your browser's user agent, and nothing you typed.
